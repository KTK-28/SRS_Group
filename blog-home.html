Brand Story · Sai Aashiyana · By SRS Group

From four walls to a lifetime of beautiful memories.

A home holds the everyday moments that slowly become the most cherished chapters of our lives.

The first quiet tea on the balcony in the morning. An unhurried Sunday family lunch in the open kitchen. The laughter of children playing safely on wide society roads. These are the small moments that turn brick and mortar into a deeply personal place of belonging.

As one of the most popular and flagship projects by SRS Group (SRS Build Venture LLP), Sai Aashiyana reflects a master vision rooted in building for a better tomorrow. With a verified developer track record of 10+ projects—including 4 completed communities, 3 active ongoing developments, and 4+ upcoming in the government approval pipeline—SRS Group brings rock-solid trust and quality craftsmanship to every brick.

Built with care in Shikargarh

Every 3BHK duplex villa is crafted with generous proportions (2,035 – 2,258 sq. ft.), high ceilings, Italian marble finishes, and natural light that flows across both levels. Backed by full JDA approval, resort-style amenities including an open-air swimming pool, mini club house, and modern gym, Sai Aashiyana is an invitation to elevate your family's standard of living.
